Experiment report · Utama + Aripin replication · AripinModified ConvNeXt update · MCT-LR + MCT-LR2 fusion · RTX 5070

Five architectures, one dataset, three protocols.

Reimplementations of Utama et al. (Attention-BiLSTM, landmark), Aripin & Setiawan (LRCN-3Conv, image), AripinModified (ConvNeXt-inspired image LRCN), MCT-LR (late-fusion multimodal), and MCT-LR2 (residual CNN + cross-attention + speaker-adversarial) on IndoLR. Each evaluated under random class-stratified, speaker-grouped, and leave-one-speaker-out protocols. 85:15 train/validation, no test set.

Executive finding

Original Aripin remains strongest overall. LRCN-3Conv achieves 98.88% random words, 99.20% random phrases, 50.93% grouped words, and 59.52% grouped phrases. AripinModified reaches 97.43% random words, 25.30% random phrases, 47.03% grouped words, and 51.90% grouped phrases. Its ConvNeXt-style backbone improves grouped phrases over MCT-LR's 34.76%, but remains below original Aripin's 59.52% and does not replace it as overall leader.

MCT-LR2 partially addresses the fusion collapse. At 7.56M parameters with residual CNN + BiLSTM image stream, motion-augmented BiGRU landmark stream, bidirectional cross-attention fusion, and speaker-adversarial training, MCT-LR2 raises grouped-word accuracy from MCT-LR's catastrophic 11.06% to 43.87%. However, random-phrase accuracy drops to 95.18% and grouped phrases regress to 28.81%, well below Aripin's 59.52%. The grouped-phrase model early-stopped at epoch 9, suggesting instability in the adversarial signal for longer sequences.

AripinModified trades random-split accuracy for grouped-phrase robustness. The 3.85M-parameter model uses depthwise 7×7 convolutions, LayerNorm, GELU, inverted bottlenecks, and a two-layer BiLSTM. Grouped phrases rise to 51.90%, while random phrases collapse to 25.30%; treat this as an architecture tradeoff, not a universal improvement.

LOSO 8-fold evaluation was introduced as a new protocol. MCT-LR2 LOSO pooled accuracy is 32.51% (words) and 27.63% (phrases), with extreme speaker-dependent variance (9.7%–59.6% per-fold for words). No existing baseline uses LOSO, so these establish the protocol; future LOSO runs of Aripin/Utama/MCT-LR are needed for comparison.

Aripin LOSO establishes an image-only unseen-speaker baseline. Under 8-fold LOSO, Aripin achieves 28.35% pooled word accuracy and 45.51% pooled phrase accuracy — the latter surpassing MCT-LR2's 27.63% by +17.88pp. Aripin's lightweight image-only LRCN generalises better to unseen phrase speakers than MCT-LR2's heavier multimodal fusion. Speaker variance remains high (word fold range: 15.80%–39.58%, phrase range: 31.50%–74.09%).

Replication configuration

  • IndoLR: 5,807 videos, 8 speakers
  • Words: 4,153 videos, 10 classes
  • Phrases: 1,654 videos, 4 classes
  • 40 lip landmarks × x/y = 80 features
  • Random/Grouped: 85:15 train/validation
  • LOSO: 8-fold, 6 train / 1 val / 1 test
  • Utama: Adam lr=0.005, wd=1e-5, batch=32, 100 epochs
  • Aripin: Adam lr=0.0005, wd=1e-5, batch=4, 100 epochs
  • MCT-LR: AdamW lr=0.001, wd=1e-4, batch=8, 150 epochs
  • AripinModified: AdamW lr=3e-4, wd=0.05, batch=4, 100 epochs, AMP

MCT-LR2 architecture

  • Image: ResNet-style 3-stage (32→64→128→256) + BiLSTM-2
  • Landmark: coordinate + inter-frame delta + BiGRU-3
  • Fusion: bidirectional cross-attention (two MHA blocks)
  • Regularisation: modality dropout 0.1, label smoothing 0.1
  • Speaker-adversarial: gradient reversal + cross-entropy
  • Data augmentation: contrast/brightness/noise (ROI), scale/translate/noise (landmarks)
  • Words: 7,564,468 params · Phrases: 7,562,926 params
  • Selected by validation macro-F1; patience 20
