Experiment report · Utama + Aripin replication · AripinModified ConvNeXt update · MCT-LR + MCT-LR2 fusion · RTX 5070

Five architectures, one dataset, three protocols.

Reimplementations of Utama et al. (Attention-BiLSTM, landmark), Aripin & Setiawan (LRCN-3Conv, image), AripinModified (ConvNeXt-inspired image LRCN), MCT-LR (late-fusion multimodal), and MCT-LR2 (residual CNN + cross-attention + speaker-adversarial) on IndoLR. Each evaluated under random class-stratified, speaker-grouped, and leave-one-speaker-out protocols. 85:15 train/validation, no test set.

Executive finding

AripinModifiedV2 is the new image-only leader. With Aripin's original 1→16→32→64 spatial backbone plus an in-model signed frame-difference motion stream and packed-sequence temporal masking, V2 achieves 97.43% random words, 97.59% random phrases, 55.86% grouped words, and 60.24% grouped phrases. It matches original Aripin on random splits and beats it by +4.93pp on grouped words and +0.72pp on grouped phrases. V2 also fixes AripinModified's random-phrase collapse (25.30% → 97.59%).

Packed-sequence masking drives +21.2pp LOSO word improvement over original Aripin. Under 8-fold LOSO, V2 word pooled accuracy reaches 49.84% (fold mean 49.95% ± 10.46%), compared to original Aripin's 28.63% and MCT-LR2's 32.68%. Every speaker fold improves, with the weakest speaker (Jelly) gaining +10.2pp and the strongest (brrr) gaining +44.5pp. V2's ability to ignore black-tail padding — a feature no prior image-only model used — removes a systematic source of cross-speaker distribution shift.

LOSO phrases tied with original Aripin. V2 phrase LOSO pooled accuracy is 45.95% (fold mean 45.54% ± 20.52%) vs Aripin's 45.83% (45.51% ± 13.21%). The motion stream helps less on longer 40-frame phrase sequences; variance remains high across speakers.

Original Aripin's full-resolution spatial backbone outperforms ConvNeXt-style pooling. AripinModified (3.85M ConvNeXt-style, global pool) regressed to 25.30% random phrases and 47.03% grouped words. V2 confirms that preserving Aripin's 64×10×10 mouth-layout map — not discarding it before temporal learning — is the correct inductive bias for IndoLR.

MCT-LR2 remains the landmark-fusion reference. At 7.56M parameters with bidirectional cross-attention and speaker-adversarial training, MCT-LR2 reaches 97.59% random words, 43.87% grouped words, and 32.68% LOSO words. V2 beats it on every grouped and LOSO cell with 2.53M parameters and no second modality.

Replication configuration

  • IndoLR: 5,807 videos, 8 speakers
  • Words: 4,153 videos, 10 classes
  • AripinModified: AdamW lr=3e-4, wd=0.05, batch=4, 100 epochs, AMP
  • AripinModifiedV2: Adam lr=5e-4, wd=1e-5, batch=4, 100 epochs, AMP
  • Random/Grouped: 85:15 train/validation
  • LOSO: 8-fold, 6 train / 1 val / 1 test
  • Utama: Adam lr=0.005, wd=1e-5, batch=32, 100 epochs
  • Aripin: Adam lr=0.0005, wd=1e-5, batch=4, 100 epochs
  • MCT-LR: AdamW lr=0.001, wd=1e-4, batch=8, 150 epochs

MCT-LR2 architecture

  • Image: ResNet-style 3-stage (32→64→128→256) + BiLSTM-2
  • Landmark: coordinate + inter-frame delta + BiGRU-3
  • Fusion: bidirectional cross-attention (two MHA blocks)
  • Regularisation: modality dropout 0.1, label smoothing 0.1
  • Speaker-adversarial: gradient reversal + cross-entropy
  • Data augmentation: contrast/brightness/noise (ROI), scale/translate/noise (landmarks)
  • Words: 7,564,468 params · Phrases: 7,562,926 params
  • Selected by validation macro-F1; patience 20
