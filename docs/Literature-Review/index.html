Bottom line

Choose LRCN-3Conv for highest reported accuracy. It reaches 95.42% on words and 95.63% on phrases in the 2024 study. The landmark Attention-BiLSTM reaches 93.03% and 95.18%, respectively, while using far less compute.

Choose Attention-BiLSTM for efficient deployment. Its reported word-level cost is 0.01 GFLOPs and 0.000530 s model inference per sample, versus 0.62 GFLOPs and 0.211583 s for LRCN-3Conv.

These are reported results, not a new controlled benchmark. The papers differ in implementation, split details, training setup, and model selection. Re-run both pipelines under one protocol before treating the margin as causal.

S1 · Image pipeline

Aripin & Setiawan · 2024 · Applied Computational Intelligence and Soft Computing
  • MediaPipe Face Mesh localizes the mouth region.
  • LRCN-3Conv is the reported best variant.
  • 95.42% word and 95.63% phrase test accuracy.
  • Higher compute: 3.30M parameters; 0.62 / 0.83 GFLOPs.
Interpretation

The image representation preserves spatial detail and wins the reported accuracy comparison. Its cost is substantially higher than coordinate-based modeling.

S2 · Landmark pipeline

Utama, Hidayat & Alfarozi · 2026 · JISEBI
  • 40 mouth landmarks become compact coordinate sequences.
  • Attention-BiLSTM reaches 93.03% word and 95.18% phrase test accuracy.
  • 0.01 / 0.14 GFLOPs and 0.000530 / 0.011366 s inference.
  • Ablation reports BiGRU+Attention at 95.10% words and 94.58% phrases.
Interpretation

Attention is the important design choice in the reported ablation. Landmark input removes most pixel redundancy, but results depend on reliable face tracking.

Evidence limits and next experiment

  1. Both direct studies report IndoLR results, but this review did not recover a shared executable benchmark or identical random seeds.
  2. The 2024 paper reports an 80:10:10 split; the 2026 paper reports its own train/validation/test procedure and also includes an ablation. Do not mix validation and test numbers.
  3. Reported accuracy is classification accuracy, not sequence transcription quality. Word and phrase tasks have different class counts and temporal difficulty.
  4. The 2025 CSA-ResNet-34 + BiGRU-Mamba result is on IDLRW: 48,083 videos and 100 words. It is related Indonesian work, not an IndoLR score, so it is excluded from the direct ranking.
Recommended project benchmark: port both reference pipelines into `src/`, freeze one speaker-wise split, train with identical seeds, report macro-F1 plus accuracy, parameter count, GFLOPs, preprocessing time, and end-to-end latency. This will test whether LRCN-3Conv’s accuracy lead survives controlled reproduction.
